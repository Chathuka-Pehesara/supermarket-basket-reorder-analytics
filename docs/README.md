## Stakeholder Engagement Summary
* **Stakeholder Profile**: Owner/Operator of an independent retail supermarket.
* **Operational Reality**: The business maintains physical shelves without digital POS systems. Decisions are traditionally made based on memory and day-to-day observation.
* **Engagement Touchpoints**:
  1. *Initial Consultation*: Problem scoping, inventory pain points, fast-moving vs. slow-moving stock discussion.
  2. *Mid-Project Check-in*: Sanity-checking generated association rules and top co-occurring itemsets.
  3. *Final Presentation & Feedback*: Review of store layout recommendations, bundling concepts, and restock prioritization.
